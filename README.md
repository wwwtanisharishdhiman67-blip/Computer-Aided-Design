# Mechanical Design & 3D CAD Portfolio

A showcase of technical 3D modeling, mechanical assemblies, and product design created using SolidWorks and Fusion AutoCAD. This repository contains the part files, assembly structures, and final renders for complex mechanical and stylized projects.

## Featured Projects

### 1. 4-Cylinder Internal Combustion Engine
A complete, fully mated 3D assembly of a 4-cylinder engine demonstrating advanced mechanical drafting and spatial reasoning.
* **Design Focus:** Precision tolerances, dynamic mating, and structural integrity.
* **Components Modeled:** Engine block, crankshaft, pistons, connecting rods, and camshafts.
* **Technical Skills Demonstrated:** Complex assembly mates, mechanical motion simulation, and top-down assembly design.

### 2. Kunai Knife - Tactical Prop Design
A precise, stylized 3D model of a traditional Kunai knife, optimized for aesthetics and manufacturability.
* **Design Focus:** Surface modeling, ergonomic handle scaling, and blade tapering.
* **Technical Skills Demonstrated:** Lofted bosses, sweep features, and aerodynamic balance rendering.
* **Use Case:** Ready for 3D printing (.STL) and digital rendering.

## Software & Tools Used
* **SolidWorks:** Primary 3D modeling, mechanical mating, and assembly.
* **Fusion AutoCAD:** Technical drafting and geometric structural planning.

## How to View
To interact with the 3D models:
1. Download the provided `.STEP` or `.IGES` files.
2. Open them in any standard 3D CAD viewer (SolidWorks, Fusion360, or free online viewers).
3. For 3D printing, use the provided `.STL` files in the respective project folders.
